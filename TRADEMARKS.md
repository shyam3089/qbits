Qbits is the project created by Shyam Mohan. The Qbits name, any project logo,
and official project identity identify the originating project. This document
does not assert that any trademark is registered, that the name is exclusive in
every context, or that a particular logo has been adopted.

The software is licensed under Apache License 2.0; see [LICENSE](LICENSE).
Section 6 addresses trademarks separately. This guidance does not change the
software license or limit rights provided by applicable trademark law, including
truthful descriptive or nominative use.

Contributors may accurately describe their contributions to Qbits and refer to
Qbits in documentation, compatibility statements, discussions and attribution.
Use an official logo in a way that accurately identifies the project and does
not imply endorsement of an independent product or service. Ask the project
maintainer before using project branding to represent an official release,
service, sponsorship or partnership.

Forks may modify, redistribute and commercialize the software under Apache 2.0.
Preserve applicable attribution and notices as the license requires, and clearly
describe an independently maintained fork and its changes. Statements such as
"based on Qbits" or "a fork of Qbits" are appropriate truthful descriptions.
Choose distinct branding for an independent project where needed to avoid
confusion about its source or endorsement. Original attribution does not require
a fork to present itself as the official project or give up its own identity.

not require permission for uses already permitted by law. Questions about uses
that would suggest official endorsement can be directed to the project creator
at smckannur@gmail.com.
